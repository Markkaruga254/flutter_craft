# Design principles

Use these principles when studying references and making interface decisions.

- **Hierarchy:** make the primary task and most important information obvious.
- **Spacing:** use a deliberate, repeatable spacing scale rather than arbitrary gaps.
- **Typography:** establish roles for display, heading, body, label and supporting text.
- **Colour:** use semantic roles and maintain sufficient contrast.
- **Consistency:** repeated patterns should behave and look alike.
- **Feedback:** actions need visible feedback; loading, empty, error and success states are part of the design.
- **Accessibility:** support readable text, meaningful labels, adequate touch targets and scalable layouts.
- **Responsive behaviour:** design for constraints and content, not one screenshot size.
- ** restraint:** animation and decoration should clarify the experience, not compete with it.

For larger projects, capture flows and visual decisions in Figma. For smaller challenges, study a reference and document what you learned before recreating it.
